---
name: manual-trading
description: "Apply general discipline to user-directed manual trading: authorization, fresh observations, decision deadlines, one guarded submission, durable receipts and evidence-based recovery."
---

Use this for a specific trade or a clearly bounded discretionary trading task. It is a manual Agent workflow, not a permanently running strategy engine.

1. Read the selected backend plugin's own prompt, skill and declared trading resources before acting. Those documents and its actual tool schemas define supported operations, parameters, account reuse, platform prerequisites and recovery steps. Within the user's authorized task, use the supported tools to install or load required plugins, prepare dependencies and configure them, reusing existing local setup. Missing setup is something to resolve, not proof that the task is impossible. Respect explicit user prohibitions, denied authorization and actual tool permissions; do not bypass them or invent trading capabilities.
2. Keep every action within the user's trading instruction and risk bounds. Installation, permissions, research and backtests do not authorize orders. Reuse existing authorization for a clearly bounded task instead of asking again for necessary checks. Do not invent quantity or price bounds.
3. Complete lengthy research before beginning a time-sensitive decision. Use M5 or longer; M1 is not enabled without measured end-to-end evidence. API response time does not measure model reasoning time. A five-minute chart does not excuse a five-minute-old decision.
4. Base the decision on a fresh source-timestamped observation of the chosen account and instrument. The decision expires after at most 60 seconds from the original quote's source time. Never replace a source timestamp or refresh merely to relabel an old decision. Reassess when expired.
5. Recheck the actual account, available position or funds, instrument constraints, price boundary, source freshness and decision expiry immediately before submission, including after queuing. New quotes may be at most 5 seconds old, and at most 1 second may pass from the final quote to submission. Submit once without another model round trip. A bounded quote does not guarantee a market fill price.
6. Use stable operation identity and preserve durable receipts. Accepted, partially filled, filled, rejected and unknown are different outcomes. A timeout or cancellation may occur after dispatch; follow the backend's own evidence-recovery procedure on the same account. Never resend with another ID or infer rejection from missing, empty or incomplete history. Zero IDs, similar remarks and unrelated account activity are not execution evidence.
7. Cancellation requires a confirmed, uniquely matched active order and a fresh ownership check. A cancellation request is not confirmation and cannot undo a fill. Closing or reducing a filled position is a separate authorized action. Follow the backend's concrete instructions for supported operations and unresolved states.

Report account, instrument, direction, quantity, requested bounds, native IDs and the actual confirmed outcome. Show source time, model decision age, quote-to-send and send-to-receipt durations separately. Identify missing evidence. Controlled fixtures do not prove live latency, profitability, zero slippage or broker-level exactly-once delivery.
